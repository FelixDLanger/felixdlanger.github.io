Research

I study how firms, assets and states create value, and how to measure it where standard metrics fall short: intangible-heavy companies, tokenized assets, sovereign wealth, and the architecture and governance that enterprise AI depends on.

Research lines

AI governance
Whether the architecture and governance that enterprise AI depends on can be observed, measured and decided on. Two working papers: Core Integrity and EAGI.
Valuation
How to value companies whose worth is mostly intangible. The Concentric Value Model.
Digital finance
What tokenization does to an asset’s cost of capital, and what blockchain means for money. TAVE, and the doctorate.
Singapore and Asian macro
How Singapore turned reserve income into fiscal infrastructure, and what its blockchain strategy means for the infrastructure behind reserve currencies. The sovereign-wealth paper, and the doctorate, which joins this line to digital finance.

Doctoral research

Doctor of Business Administration, GlobalNxt University, expected 2027.

Working title: From Tigers to Tokens: Singapore’s Blockchain Strategy and the Future of Reserve Currency Infrastructure.

The thesis follows Singapore from the Asian Tiger era to its blockchain strategy today, and asks what role blockchain and tokenization could play in the infrastructure behind reserve currencies. It combines expert interviews with a quantitative analysis of reserve-composition data.
