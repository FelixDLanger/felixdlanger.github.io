Felix Diego Langer

Enterprise architect and researcher in AI governance, valuation and digital finance.

I work on enterprise transformation and AI adoption at SAP in Bangkok. My research follows value across three scales: firms, tokenized assets, and Singapore’s sovereign reserves. My doctorate joins the last two, asking what blockchain means for the infrastructure behind reserve currencies. And I build tools to test each idea.
