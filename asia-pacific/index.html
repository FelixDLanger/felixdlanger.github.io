Concierge notes

  • Hungry Hub sells fixed-price packages that are often the cheapest way into omakase counters and hotel buffets.
  • Sorn releases tables at 12:00 on the 25th of each month for the following month; set a reminder.
  • Buddhist holidays bring a nationwide ban on alcohol sales; plan tasting menus with wine pairings around them.
What to buy in ThailandTailoring, crafts and massage
  • Tailored suits and shirts in Bangkok, ready in days. Mine come from Signature's Collection on Sukhumvit.
  • Local crafts, ceramics and design at markets and concept stores.
  • Massage and spa time at a fraction of European prices; see the Spa & wellness places.

Tax refundVAT refunds for tourists on 2,000 baht or more per store per day. Take the goods out within 60 days and show them to customs before check-in.

Before you fly

Booking and entry notes collected along the way.

Fly

  • Phuket sits on the Andaman coast and Koh Samui in the Gulf of Thailand. When the Andaman monsoon brings afternoon storms (roughly May to October), Phuket's top resorts discount hardest while Samui stays drier.
  • Bangkok Airways dominates Bangkok–Samui, so that route carries a fare premium. Midweek departures tend to price lowest.
  • When fuel prices spike, regional fares rise and schedules thin out. Book short-haul hops early instead of waiting for a drop.

Book

  • Compare an online travel agency plus cashback with booking direct on loyalty status. At the same net cost, pick the better location and the newer renovation.
  • In Bangkok, Hungry Hub's fixed-price packages are often the cheapest way into omakase counters.
  • In China, Trip.com handles trains, observation decks and attraction tickets with a foreign card.

Pay & connect in China

  • Set up a VPN before you land; once you're on Chinese networks it is nearly impossible.
  • Link a Visa or Mastercard to Alipay's international version before you go. It covers most merchants, and DiDi also takes foreign cards.
  • Keep restaurants' Chinese names for DiDi drivers. Every card with a local name has a copy button.

Eat

  • Night markets and mall food courts are where the region eats well for little. In Phuket, get a fresh avocado shake at the night market.
  • Many mall food courts still run on a prepaid card or coupons from the counter; cash in what you don't use before you leave.

Entry & timing

  • Philippines: complete eTravel within 72 hours before arrival.
  • China: as of October 2026, German passport holders enter visa-free for up to 30 days under a scheme running to 31 December 2026. Check before booking.
  • Thailand: Buddhist holidays bring a nationwide ban on alcohol sales.
  • Sakura: peak bloom lasts about a week. Kyoto usually holds a day or two longer than Osaka, and higher ground blooms later.
